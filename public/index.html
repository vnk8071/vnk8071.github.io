/FAQ
N. 07
What kind of AI work do you take on?
Computer vision and edge deployment: model optimisation, C++ inference runtimes, and the service around them. I am most useful when a model has to run on real hardware with a latency budget.
Which hardware and runtimes do you know?
Qualcomm QNN (HTP) on Snapdragon, TensorRT and ONNX Runtime on NVIDIA, ExecuTorch and TFLite on mobile, llama.cpp on CPU, plus CUDA, Vulkan and Hexagon device selection.
Can you improve an existing model instead of starting over?
Usually yes. Profiling first: find where the time and memory actually go, then decide between graph changes, quantisation, a different runtime, or a smaller architecture.
Are you open to freelance work or a new role?
I am open to conversations about Edge AI, inference runtimes and AI products. Email is the fastest way to reach me; I reply within one business day.
How should we start?
Send the model or task, the target hardware, and what success looks like. A repository, a benchmark number or a failing profile is even better.
