Privacy

What this site measures, and what it does not.

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts, newsletter or advertising, and it does not try to identify visitors.

Traffic is measured with Google Analytics 4, but only after you press Accept on the cookie notice. Before that, no request is made to Google: no script, no cookie, no pixel. Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control counts as a decline, and the banner is not shown.

After consent, Google Analytics records the page you view, an approximate location derived from your IP address, your device and browser type, and the events this site sends. It is used only to see which pages are useful.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and Instagram leave this site; those services have their own policies and cookies.

If you email me, the conversation stays in my inbox. Questions: nguyenkhoi8071@gmail.com.
