# Kilomet 🏃

[**Live**](https://kilomet.com.vn)

Kilomet is a place to share and connect, accompanying runners on their running
journey: personalized training plans, on-trail sessions with coaches, and a
team to race with, from Sa Pa to Chamonix. The site is available in Vietnamese
and English.

## Highlights
- Coach, club (CLB), race and blog sections
- Personalized training plan sign-up
- Bilingual (VI / EN) with a light / dark theme
